## The roast

> They are writing the law that will govern artificial intelligence, yet they still need an assistant to turn a Word document into a PDF — the future of technology, regulated by people who cannot find the Export button.

1 law for AI, 0 PDFs converted, 1 Export button missing: the sentence opens with the highest possible stakes — lawmakers writing the law that will govern artificial intelligence — then drops to the most basic file task in the office, a Word document that still needs an assistant to become a PDF, and lands the punch by putting the two together: the future of technology, regulated by people who cannot find the Export button. It is funny because the contrast is exact — the people deciding what AI may do cannot do what a menu item does.
